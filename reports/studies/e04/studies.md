## Exchangeability of validation and test normals

`shift AUROC` = P(a test normal scores above a validation normal); 0.5 under exchangeability — a test of the *bulk*. `exceed p` is the exact permutation p-value of at least this many test normals above the validation threshold if validation and test normals were exchangeable — a test of the *upper tail*, which is what the threshold depends on. Seeds share their images, so their p-values are not independent. `FPR 95% UB` is the Clopper-Pearson upper bound of the realized rate.
